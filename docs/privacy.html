Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the QIK app ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your academic data is handled securely.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Google Account Information

When you log in to the app, we use Google Sign-In to authenticate you. We may collect your email address and profile name to verify your identity strictly for authentication purposes.

1.2 Academic Data

To provide the app's core functionality—such as displaying attendance, marks, and timetables—we fetch data associated with your student profile from the official institute portal (nbkrist.org). This includes:

  • Roll number
  • Attendance records
  • Mid-term marks and semester results
  • Course information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing you with a personalized dashboard of your college activities
  • Displaying your academic progress (attendance, marks)
  • Enabling features like the student leaderboard (if you opt in)
  • Sending important campus notifications

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your academic data is displayed only to you within the app. Leaderboard features may display your name and limited academic stats (like attendance percentage) to other users, but strictly within the context of the app's gamification features.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your academic data is fetched securely via HTTPS. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in the app
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of leaderboard features at any time
